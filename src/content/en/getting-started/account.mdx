# Account and security

Your account holds your orders, wallet and role permissions. Check the signed-in account before acting, especially when switching between buyer, merchant and administrator accounts on one device.

## Registration and sign-in

1. Open the [sign-in page](https://tdcloud.cc/login).
2. Sign in with your account credentials. GitHub sign-in may also be available when configured.
3. To create an account, use the registration link shown on the page. The platform controls whether registration is open.
4. Open Profile and check your username and user number.

The GitHub sign-in option depends on platform configuration. To connect GitHub to an existing platform account, see [Connected accounts](/en/getting-started/connections/).

## Editing your profile

Update the editable fields in Profile, save and check the result. Your user number identifies your account; changing a display name does not create a different account.

You can give support your user number to locate an account issue. Do not share passwords, sign-in tokens or complete payment credentials.

## Changing your password

Under Settings → Account Security, enter your current password, new password and confirmation, then submit. The form checks required fields, matching confirmation and whether the new password differs from the old one.

This form cannot reset a forgotten current password. Use any recovery option actually provided on the sign-in page, or contact platform support. Do not include your old password in a support request.

## Roles and access

One account can have user, merchant and developer capabilities. Merchant and developer applications require their respective invitations; approved roles determine which centers appear.

If access is denied, check your account, invitation and review status, then refresh or sign in again. Editing the browser URL does not grant a role. See [Platform and roles](/en/getting-started/).

## Leaving a shared device

Sign out through Settings or the user menu. Change your password if it may have been exposed. If a developer Client Secret was exposed, [rotate that Secret](/en/developer/oauth-client/) in the Developer Center instead; it is a separate credential.
