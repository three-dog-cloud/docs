# Your first visit

Sign in to the [user dashboard](https://tdcloud.cc/dashboard), then follow this path to your first purchase. Merchants and developers can move to their own guides after preparing an account.

## 1. Prepare your account

When registration is open, the sign-in page shows a registration link. If it is unavailable, contact platform support about obtaining an account; a missing link does not necessarily indicate a page error.

Check which account is signed in under Profile, then review your password and notification preferences under Settings. See [Account and security](/en/getting-started/account/).

## 2. Choose a product

Open the Shop and read the product description, purchase instructions, price, stock and per-order quantity limit. If additional fields appear, check what delivery information the merchant needs before completing them.

The product currency can differ from the payment currency. After choosing a payment method, use the final amount shown in the confirmation area.

## 3. Prepare payment

- For TDC, check your available wallet balance. If needed, [top up](/en/currency/topup/) first.
- For online payment, choose an enabled channel supported by the product and review its quote.
- If no channel is available, see the checks in [Buying products](/en/shopping/).

A top-up and a product purchase are separate transactions. A successful top-up increases your balance; it does not automatically pay a product order.

## 4. Complete and check the order

Submit the order once and keep its order number. Open it under My Orders to pay and check payment and delivery progress. A browser redirect, success message or closed payment window alone does not establish that delivery is complete.

See [Orders, payment and delivery](/en/shopping/orders/) for statuses and [Wallet and transactions](/en/currency/wallet/) for balance changes.
