# Account Top-up

An account top-up purchases TDC through an external payment channel. The page displays the complete cost and expected credit before payment.

## Flow

1. Open Online Top-up and select an available channel.
2. Select a preset amount or enter a custom amount.
3. Review the top-up amount, fee, total payable, and expected TDC credit.
4. Start the payment and complete it on the payment page.
5. After confirmation, check the wallet or top-up history for the result.

The page shows the amount range supported by the selected channel. Available channels, currencies, and fees are those displayed at the time of payment.

## Page fields

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Top-up amount | Amount used to purchase TDC |
| Fee | Additional charge for this top-up |
| Total payable | Top-up amount plus the fee |
| TDC credit | TDC expected in the wallet after successful payment |

## Temporarily unavailable

Payment remains unavailable if the channel cannot be used, the amount is invalid, or the expected TDC credit cannot currently be displayed. Choose another available channel, adjust the amount, or try again later.

Do not pay outside the platform when no expected credit is shown. Start each top-up from the current Online Top-up page.

## Checking the result

A top-up purchases TDC; it does not automatically pay a product order. After closing the payment window or returning to the platform, check confirmation in Top-up Records, then match the wallet credit and transaction.

If the provider charged you but the record is still unconfirmed, keep the top-up identifier and receipt and ask the platform to investigate before paying again. See [Wallet and transactions](/en/currency/wallet/) for redemption codes and related records.
