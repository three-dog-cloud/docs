# Amounts and Fees

Top-ups and product orders may include fees. Before paying, review the transaction amount, fee, and total payable separately.

## Top-up fees

> **Total payable = top-up amount + fee**

The fee is normally added to the top-up amount and is not deducted from the expected TDC credit shown on the page. For example:

| Item | Amount or note |
| --- | ---: |
| Top-up amount | `10.00 USD` |
| Fee | `4.56 USD` |
| **Total payable** | **`14.56 USD`** |
| Expected credit | As shown on the confirmation page |

## Product order fees

The payment confirmation page lists:

- the product subtotal;
- the buyer fee;
- the final payable amount;
- the payment currency.

If the product price and payment channel use different currencies, the confirmation page shows the converted payable amount. Use the final amount displayed there.

## Historical amounts

Top-up history and order details retain the amount and currency confirmed for the transaction. If a payment result has not updated or an amount looks incorrect, do not pay again; contact platform support with the order number.
