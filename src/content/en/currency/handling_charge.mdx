# Amounts and Fees

Top-ups, product orders and OpenAPI collections may include fees. Before paying, review the transaction amount, fee, and total payable separately.

## Top-up fees

> **Total payable = top-up amount + fee**

The fee is normally added to the top-up amount and is not deducted from the expected TDC credit shown on the page. For example:

| Item | Amount or note |
| --- | ---: |
| Top-up amount | `10.00 USD` |
| Fee | `4.56 USD` |
| **Total payable** | **`14.56 USD`** |
| Expected credit | As shown on the confirmation page |

## Product order fees

The payment confirmation page lists:

- the product subtotal;
- the buyer fee;
- the final payable amount;
- the payment currency.

If the product price and payment channel use different currencies, the confirmation page shows the converted payable amount. Use the final amount displayed there.

## Merchant proceeds

For platform-channel collection, buyer fees increase the payer total and merchant fees reduce settlement proceeds. Net proceeds are credited in TDC; buyer fees are excluded from merchant income. Merchant-owned channels receive funds directly, without a second TDC sales credit.

Amounts and fees are rounded in the selected payment currency. A positive fee has a minimum equal to the method’s smallest chargeable amount. The displayed payer total is used for the provider charge. Currencies without a supported amount format are not offered.

## Refunds and duplicate notifications

Full refunds are verified against the original payer total. TDC accounting uses the saved exchange rate. Fees are nonrefundable, so refunds can exceed net proceeds; guarantee and other funds cover the difference under platform rules. Partial refunds are not automatically processed as full-order refunds.

Only verified payment confirms a credit. Returning to a page or clicking a success button is not evidence of payment. Duplicate notifications do not credit twice, and replaying the original payment notification after a refund does not settle the order again.

## Historical amounts

Top-up history and order details retain the amount and currency confirmed for the transaction. If a payment result has not updated or an amount looks incorrect, do not pay again; contact platform support with the order number.
