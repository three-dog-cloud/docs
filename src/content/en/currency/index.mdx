# TDC Balance

TDC is the account balance unit used by TD Cloud for marketplace purchases, order payments, and settlement. It is not a token on a public blockchain.

## Getting TDC

You can purchase TDC through Online Top-up. After you choose a payment channel and amount, the confirmation panel shows:

- the top-up amount;
- the fee;
- the total payable;
- the expected TDC credit.

Review these values before paying. TDC is added to your platform wallet after the payment channel confirms the transaction.

## Expected credit

Payment channels may use different currencies. The page calculates and displays the TDC you will receive, so you do not need to convert it yourself.

If a channel cannot currently provide an expected credit, payment remains unavailable. Choose another available channel or try again later.

## Amount display

Payment amounts use the appropriate currency format, while TDC balances may display up to eight decimal places. Transaction records retain the amounts confirmed at the time of payment.

See [Amounts and Fees](/en/currency/handling_charge/) for details about fees and the total payable.
