# Merchant onboarding and operations

The Merchant Center manages your store, products and orders. A user account must receive a merchant invitation, submit an application and pass review before gaining access to merchant operations.

## Prepare your store information

| Field | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Store name | Required; use the name customers should see |
| Business scope | Required; describe the products or services you sell |
| Logo URL | Optional; use an accessible image address |
| Short description and introduction | Explain product use, delivery and support |
| Website and public contact | Optional; provide details you are willing to publish |

Store information is used publicly. Do not put internal administration URLs, payment keys or private credentials in these fields.

## Application and review

1. After receiving an invitation, open Merchant Application from the user menu.
2. Complete your store information and submit.
3. Track review progress on the application page. Pending applicants cannot operate products.
4. If rejected, read the reason, revise and resubmit.
5. Once approved and active, manage your store through the Merchant Center.

Application history and current merchant status are separate. A previously approved merchant can be disabled, so a historical approval does not establish current operating access.

## Start operating

1. Check your public store information.
2. [Configure products](/en/merchant/products/), including prices, stock, purchase requirements and payment channels.
3. Read [Payment and settlement](/en/merchant/payments/) to distinguish buyer charges from merchant revenue.
4. Check the product, ordering flow and delivery instructions from a buyer's perspective.
5. Use [Merchant orders and reconciliation](/en/merchant/orders/) to match orders with wallet credits.

A custom application that reads products or places orders for users also needs a separate [developer application](/en/developer/quickstart/). Merchant approval does not automatically create an OAuth client.

## Current availability

Store profiles, product management, merchant orders and settlement through platform channels are available. Merchant-owned payment channels are not open. A wallet balance does not imply that withdrawals, bank payouts or self-service refunds are available in the interface.
